Your choices

Delete your Watchful data.

No app account is required. You can remove local data yourself and contact us about publisher-held records.

On your device

  1. In Settings, choose “Pause all AI checks”. Requests already sent may finish.
  2. Remove each saved API key in Settings. This removes the local copy; revoke the key separately in your AI provider account if needed. Remove keys before uninstalling to ensure their removal from iOS Keychain.
  3. Delete watchers to remove their saved history, current session result and check metadata. Gemini results are otherwise held only in memory until the app process closes; OpenAI history remains saved locally. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes other local app data, subject to device backups.

Purchase and support records

Email info@jamescullimore.dev with the subject “Watchful data deletion” for publisher-held support or purchase records and RevenueCat end-user data processed on our behalf. Provide only enough information to locate the relevant record. Never send API keys, passwords, card details or private watcher content.

Deleting local data does not remove records held by AI providers or app stores. Contact those providers for their records. Records that must be retained by law may remain; deleting data does not itself request a refund.

Support email is handled in Google Workspace (Gmail). Requesting deletion of support mail does not cancel a purchase or revoke an API key. We may ask for proportionate information to locate your records and verify the request, and will explain any records that need to be retained.

Read the Android policy or iOS policy for details.
