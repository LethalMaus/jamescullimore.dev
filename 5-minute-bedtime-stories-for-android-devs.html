5 Minute Bedtime Stories
For Android Devs

A collection of short bedtime stories for developers: funny, slightly unbelievable, and occasionally suspiciously specific.

This book began, like many good ideas, late at night. After long days at conferences, meetups, and talks, a group of developers kept swapping stories that only make sense if you have lived through them: bugs, strange systems, questionable decisions, and the occasional disaster.

At some point, it became a running joke that these stories deserved to be written down. Naturally, the only reasonable next step was to actually do it.

About the book

Conference stories, production mistakes, and developer folklore

Some stories in this book are funny. Some are slightly unbelievable. Some are close enough to the truth that a few people may start feeling uncomfortable.

Not every story is guaranteed to be entirely true, but that is part of the point. As the Irish say: “Never let the truth get in the way of a good story.”

To protect both the innocent and the guilty, the authors are not matched to their stories. You are free to guess who wrote what.

This book would not exist without the people behind it. What started as a joke quickly turned into something real thanks to developers willing to share the funny ones, the painful ones, and the ones that probably should not have happened in the first place.

Each story comes from lived experience, or at least something close enough to it. A sincere thank you to everyone who contributed their time, creativity, and stories.

What you get

A short, free read made for Android developers

The goal is simple: a free digital book you can open immediately, skim in one sitting, and share with other Android developers who have seen enough production incidents to appreciate the joke.

If you have ever come back from a conference dinner with a story that should probably never be repeated in a postmortem, this book is for you.
