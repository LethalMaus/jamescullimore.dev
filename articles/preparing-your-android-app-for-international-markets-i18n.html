Preparing your Android app for international markets (i18n)

By James Cullimore • Aug 10, 2023 • 4 min read

In today’s interconnected world, catering to a diverse user base is essential for the success of any mobile application. Android Internationalization (i18n) is the key to adapting your app to different languages, regions, and cultural nuances. In this article, we will delve into the intricacies of i18n and explore the challenges it presents, along with effective strategies to overcome them.

Difference between Internationalization (i18n) and Localization (l10n):

Before diving into the details, let’s briefly differentiate between i18n and l10n. While localization focuses on adapting an application to a specific locale, including language translations, date formats, and currency symbols, i18n encompasses a broader perspective. i18n involves designing and implementing the application in a way that makes it easier to localize, taking into account the cultural differences, text directionality, and other regional variations.

Considerations and Best Practices:

When venturing into Android i18n, there are several crucial aspects to consider in order to provide a seamless user experience across different locales.

  1. One significant consideration is the support for right-to-left (RTL) languages. RTL languages require adjustments in layout and alignment to ensure proper rendering and readability. Text expansion is another challenge, as languages like French often result in longer strings compared to English. Developers must carefully consider the impact of such expansions on the UI/UX, ensuring that text does not overlap or get truncated.
  2. Hard-coded strings can cause issues during localization updates. It’s essential to externalize all user-facing strings into resource files, making them easily translatable without the need to modify the code. Furthermore, attention should be given to text formatting, such as decimal placements and commas, which can vary across languages.
  3. Time zones play a crucial role in internationalization, especially when dealing with date and time-related functionalities. Ensuring accurate and appropriate time zone conversions and formatting is vital for providing a seamless experience to users worldwide.
  4. Text encoding is another crucial consideration. Adopting Unicode-based encodings like UTF-8 allows support for a wide range of characters and ensures compatibility across different languages and scripts.

Store Releases and Language Support:

While making your app available in various countries can expand your user base, it is crucial to release the app only in countries where supported languages and formats are available. Failing to do so may lead to negative reviews and user dissatisfaction. Prioritize releasing your app in countries where you can provide a localized experience that meets the expectations of users.

Leveraging App Bundles for Dynamic Localization:

App Bundles present an effective solution to reduce app size and streamline the delivery of localized content. By utilizing app bundles, you can dynamically download the correct string XML files tailored to the user’s device language. This approach allows users to receive only the necessary resources, resulting in a more efficient and personalized app experience.

UI Testing and Localization Discrepancies:

To ensure consistency and quality across localized versions, conducting thorough UI testing is crucial. Espresso, a popular testing framework, can be utilized to take screenshots of each TextView and its corresponding string resource pair for every supported language. This approach enables the identification of any UI discrepancies, alignment issues, or text overflow. Furthermore, tests can be configured to fail when encountering ellipsized text or text that exceeds the boundaries of the views, ensuring optimal user experience across all locales. Translation and Over-the-Air Updates:

In the process of internationalizing your app, leveraging translation services and tools can significantly ease the burden of managing translations. Services such as Phrase and Lokalise provide comprehensive localization platforms that assist in organizing, translating, and maintaining multiple language resources. Additionally, they offer features like over-the-air updates, enabling seamless updates to translated content without requiring a new app release. Conclusion:

Android Internationalization (i18n) plays a vital role in providing a localized and culturally sensitive experience to users worldwide. By understanding the nuances and challenges involved, implementing best practices, and utilizing effective tools and services, you can ensure your app seamlessly adapts to different languages and regions, expanding your global reach and user satisfaction. Embrace i18n, and unlock the potential of your Android app on a global scale.
