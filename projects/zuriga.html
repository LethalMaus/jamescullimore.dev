Zuriga

A connected coffee machine app, rebuilt across Android and iOS.

Kotlin MultiplatformIoTAndroid & iOS
Project links ↓

The brief

An existing Kotlin Multiplatform app connected users to their coffee machine. The brief was to take over the app, replace its interface, and improve the business logic around Bluetooth Low Energy communication.

What we delivered

  • Took over the existing codebase, working largely independently with support from the design team.
  • Rebuilt the complete user interface across Android and iOS using Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform.
  • Improved the BLE-related business logic used to communicate with the connected machine.

The result

A fully rewritten interface with a simpler, more modern product feel across Android and iOS, alongside improvements to the machine-communication logic.
