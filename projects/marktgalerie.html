LTE Consulting / Marktmeister Pro

Development across around five apps, including Marktgalerie and Markttagmeister.

AndroidProduct deliveryTechnical review
Project links ↓

The project

A collaboration with LTE Consulting covering around five applications. The work included Marktgalerie for event photo documentation, Markttagmeister for on-site event organisation, and SeeQRe for scanning and decoding QR codes. Marktgalerie began with a prototype audit and consulting before expanding into further development.

The contribution

  • Contributed Android development, architecture, app design and feature implementation across the app portfolio.
  • Reviewed the Marktgalerie prototype and took on further development of its photo documentation features.
  • Worked on apps supporting event organisation and QR-code scanning.

From the team

“As part of the app development project Marktgalerie, aimed at assisting market and festival planners in documenting their events through photos, we had the pleasure of collaborating with James. James initially served as an auditor and consultant for the existing prototype. Due to his high level of technical expertise, his dependable and fast assistance, and his ability to convey complex technical concepts with precision, we decided during the course of the project to entrust him with further development of the app. This decision was not one we regretted. In a very short time, together with James, we were able to create an app that provides our clients with comprehensive support for photo documentation. We look forward to our future projects and the opportunities to work with James once again.”

Kira Stolley, Marktmeister Pro
