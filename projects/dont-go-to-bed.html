Don’t Go To Bed

A family adventure made with my sons, Leo and Ian.

Family gameAndroid & iOS
Project links ↓

The project

Explore, build and find Mammy before nightfall. The game grew out of the boys’ ideas, with playable family characters and a new world each session. Available on Android, iPhone and iPad.

Inside the product

  • Break and collect blocks to build a route through the world.
  • Explore from different directions and play together on a trusted local Wi-Fi network.
  • Free gameplay, no ads and no game account; optional support tips sit behind a grown-ups gate.
