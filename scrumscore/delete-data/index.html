Delete your ScrumScore data

  1. Open Your team.
  2. Open Account & administration.
  3. Choose Delete my account and data, then confirm.

This permanently deletes the household’s player profiles, predictions, memberships and club requests. It affects every player managed by that account. Groups may remain for other members. Limited administrative audit records and backups may have separate retention.

Lost access? Contact support with your account ID if available. We need to verify ownership before deleting data. Do not send children’s personal details.
