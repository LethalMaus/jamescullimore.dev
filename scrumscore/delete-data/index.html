Delete your ScrumScore data

In the app

  1. Open Your team.
  2. Open Account & administration.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This deletes the household’s player profiles and predictions. It affects every player managed by that account. An existing group may remain for other members.

If you cannot access the app

Contact support. Include your app account ID if you have it; we may need to verify ownership before deleting data. Do not send a password or details about children.

Subscriptions

Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Manage or cancel any subscription separately in the store where you purchased it.
