A little help with ScrumScore

Need help with a group invitation, a prediction or a result? Tell us the app version, device and what happened. Don’t send passwords, private invite codes or children’s personal information.

Email support

One player, one invitation

Each invitation admits one player and expires after 14 days. If it has already been used or expired, ask your organiser for a new one.

Saved is not submitted

An offline draft stays on your device. Reconnect and submit before the deadline. Only a server-confirmed prediction counts.

Your household currently belongs to this installation. Reinstalling or clearing app data can lose access. Cross-device recovery is not available yet.
