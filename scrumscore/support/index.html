ScrumScore support

Need help with your household, a group invitation or a match result?

Email support

Include the app version and a description of the problem. Never send passwords, payment-card details or a child’s personal information.

Joining a group

Each invitation admits one player. If it has already been used or expired, ask your group organizer for a new one. A parent can add a child on the same device.

Moving to another device

Player profiles currently belong to the anonymous account on the installation. Reinstalling can lose access to those profiles. Cross-device household recovery is not available yet.
