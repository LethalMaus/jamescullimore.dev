ScrumScore free-beta terms

Review edition · 13 October 2026

A game to share

ScrumScore is operated by James Cullimore, Auf der Nuß 9, 78078 Niedereschach, Germany. It is a private rugby prediction game for families, friends and clubs. Adult organisers manage their households and invitations and supervise children’s participation. Contact info@jamescullimore.dev for help.

Free means free

This beta requires no payment or subscription and does not automatically turn into a paid plan. If paid features are introduced later, their price and terms will be shown before you choose to purchase. A club or league request is an enquiry, not an order or a promise of inclusion in a future release.

Playing fairly

Choose a suitable nickname and keep invitation codes private. Each invitation admits one player and expires after 14 days. Do not harass other players, misuse the service or include someone else’s personal information. ScrumScore does not accept stakes, take bets or pay out winnings. Any optional friendly prize description is arranged by the group organiser, not supplied or guaranteed by ScrumScore; do not use the app for unlawful betting.

Predictions and results

Submit predictions before the server’s deadline. A draft saved on a device does not count until the server confirms submission. Points are calculated from published results; administrators can correct fixtures or results and points may change. ScrumScore is not an official competition results service. Fixture times, availability and live coverage are not guaranteed.

What to expect from the beta

The beta is unfinished and may change or be interrupted. Report problems through support. Household access currently depends on this installation; clearing app data or reinstalling can lose access. Keep this limitation in mind before relying on the app for a season. You can stop using the app and request account deletion at any time. Nothing in these terms removes mandatory consumer rights or limits liability where the law does not allow it.
