ScrumScore app privacy

Free beta · 31 October 2026

Who looks after your data

James Cullimore, Auf der Nuß 9, 78078 Niedereschach, Germany, is responsible for ScrumScore. Contact info@jamescullimore.dev about privacy or your data. This notice covers the app; the separate website privacy notice covers this website.

The short version

Pick a nickname, not your full name. Your household has a private account and each player has a profile. People in your group can see your nickname and ranking. Other players’ predictions are hidden until the prediction deadline. There are no public player profiles, chat or third-party advertisements. An adult should help children choose nicknames and manage invitations. Do not put addresses, schools or other identifying details in names, prizes or club requests.

What the app stores

We store a household identifier, authentication sessions, player nicknames, private groups, membership, invitations, predictions and points to run the game. An adult can optionally send a club enquiry containing a contact email and description. Administrators use email-based accounts; administrative changes produce audit records. Basic network and security information, including IP addresses, may be processed by our hosting provider when the app connects. No email is required for an ordinary player profile. Invited adults can also use an email-based household account to recover their profiles on Android and iOS. Passwords and verification links are handled by Supabase Auth.

Why we use it

Account and gameplay data provide the service requested by the adult household organiser (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where applicable). Security, abuse prevention and administrative audits protect the service and its users (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests). Optional adult enquiries are handled to respond to that request, including steps towards a possible agreement. Children’s interests require particular protection; use only the minimum nickname-based information for a child’s participation. No advertising profile or legally significant automated decision is made. Game points are calculated automatically from predictions and published results.

Your device and our providers

Supabase hosts the app database in its EU West region (Ireland) and provides authentication. Authorised service providers may process operational data; international processing is subject to the applicable provider safeguards and data-processing agreement. The app keeps a protected local session, cached game data and offline drafts. Optional match reminders are scheduled on your device; you can disable them in device settings. RevenueCat integration is disabled for this free beta: there is no subscription checkout. A future paid version will need updated information before billing is enabled.

Deletion and retention

Gameplay data remains while your account is used. In Your team → Account & administration, you can delete the household account, its players, predictions, memberships and club requests. This affects everyone sharing that account. Shared groups may remain for other members; please avoid personal details in shared text. A minimal account identifier stays in a deletion queue until external customer deletion is confirmed. Administrative audit history and provider security logs have separate retention. Recovery copies, when made, must be protected and expire under the operator’s retention policy; restoring a copy must not undo a deletion request. Uninstalling alone does not delete server data. Administrative audit entries are deleted after 90 days. Completed or declined club enquiries are deleted 90 days after closure; reopening an enquiry resets that clock.

Your choices and rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact info@jamescullimore.dev; we may need to verify account ownership without collecting unnecessary information. You can complain to a data-protection authority, including the authority in Baden-Württemberg. Supplying a nickname and the basic account data is necessary to play; club enquiries and reminders are optional. Anonymous access stays tied to this installation until you sign in. In version 0.1.1 and later, invited adults can sign into the same household account on another device; signing in moves existing anonymous profiles into that account.
