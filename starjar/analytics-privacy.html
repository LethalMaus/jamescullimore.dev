StarJar: optional analytics

Optional usage analytics

Where available, a parent can choose to share usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase. This is off by default. Choosing not to share does not limit StarJar features. Change your choice in Parent Settings at any time.

If enabled, Firebase processes an app-installation identifier, app and device information, approximate location derived from network information, and usage/session and purchase events. StarJar records setup completion, the first observed approved task and redeemed reward, Premium views and purchase outcomes. These technical data are pseudonymous, not anonymous. We do not send profile names, PINs, task or reward text, Google Drive contents or your email address as analytics events.

We disable advertising identifier collection, advertising consent and personalised-ad signals for this feature. We do not use these events to build advertising audiences. Google processes analytics under its applicable service and data-processing terms; processing may take place outside your country. See Firebase privacy information. The legal basis for this optional analytics is your consent.

Turning analytics off stops future collection by this feature and resets the local analytics identifier and stored analytics data. It does not automatically erase previously received server data or aggregated reports. For deletion questions contact info@jamescullimore.dev. RevenueCat purchase processing, optional cloud sync and support correspondence are separate from this choice.
