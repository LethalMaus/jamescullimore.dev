Before your first star

How do tasks and rewards work?

Agree on a task and how many stars it earns. Your child marks it complete, you approve it, and they can save their stars for a reward you choose together.

Does StarJar work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. StarJar is available on Google Play for Android and the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Can we use it on more than one device?

Premium includes optional family sync. Parents connect the same supported cloud account on each device. Sync runs while the app is open; simultaneous changes may need a parent to choose which version to keep.

Do we need an account to start?

You can use StarJar locally. A cloud account is only needed if you choose to enable optional family sync.
