Chores become stars. Kids stay motivated. Parents stay in control.

Before your first star

Is StarJar free?

StarJar has a free plan to get started. Optional Premium adds unlimited tasks, unlimited rewards and multiple child profiles. Available plans and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do chores and rewards work?

Create a task and choose how many stars it earns. Your child marks it complete, you review it, and they can save their stars for rewards you choose together, such as a film night or an ice cream treat.

Can more than one child use StarJar?

Yes. Premium supports multiple child profiles so you can keep each child’s tasks and progress separate.

Can children use it before they can read?

Tasks and rewards have read-aloud support. Parents set up the routine and review completed chores, with a PIN protecting the parent area.

Is StarJar available on Android and iPhone?
