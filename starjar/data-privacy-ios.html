StarJar Data privacy

iOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026.

Who we are and who this policy covers

This policy covers StarJar for iPhone and iPad, provided by James Cullimore – Software Engineering. StarJar helps parents and children manage family chores, stars and rewards. Parents set up and manage the app. Contact: info@jamescullimore.dev. See our legal notice for provider details.

The Android app has a separate privacy policy. Visiting this website is also subject to our website privacy information.

Family information stays on your device

Family profile names, avatars, parent PINs, chores, rewards, stars and activity history are stored in the app’s local device storage. There is no StarJar cloud account or family-data synchronization service. We do not send this family content to RevenueCat. Device backups may be handled by Apple according to your device settings.

Children can use profiles created by a parent. Use nicknames rather than full names if preferred. Keep the parent PIN private. A PIN protects parent controls; it does not make the local storage an encrypted vault.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes App Store payments and manages subscriptions. StarJar uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain Premium access, restore purchases and provide purchase reporting. RevenueCat is initialized when the app launches, including before a purchase. It uses an anonymous app identifier and receives purchase history, receipt/transaction information and technical information such as operating system and device details, app version and activity timestamps. Network requests also expose connection information such as an IP address to the receiving service. An anonymous identifier is not a promise that all information is aggregated.

StarJar does not provide family names, PINs, chore descriptions, reward descriptions or an email-based account identifier to RevenueCat. We do not receive your payment-card number through the app. The parent PIN is required before the in-app purchase screen, restoration controls and its external legal links are available.

RevenueCat describes using TLS for transport and AWS data centers in the United States for storage. Processing on our behalf is subject to our agreements with RevenueCat and its data-processing terms. Read RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and external services

The iOS app does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. It has no third-party advertising and does not request an advertising identifier. RevenueCat’s purchase reporting remains in use. The iOS app does not show the YouTube trailer. Links to support, the privacy policy and Apple’s terms open external services with their own privacy practices. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include; please avoid sending children’s personal information.

Why information is processed

Local family information is used to provide the features you configure. Purchase-related information is used to deliver and restore paid access, prevent purchase fraud and understand purchase performance. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request. Where applicable, we rely on performance of the requested service, legal obligations, and legitimate interests in reliable purchase administration and support. We do not use a child’s local profile as an advertising or marketing account.

Retention, deletion and subscription cancellation

In the parent area, Settings → Clear All Data removes local profiles, PINs, chores, rewards and progress after confirmation. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data subject to your device’s backup behavior. Neither action cancels subscriptions or deletes Apple or RevenueCat transaction records. Apple may continue renewals and purchase-related server processing after uninstalling.

Cancel recurring subscriptions in your Apple subscription settings. For help accessing or deleting purchase-related information or support correspondence, contact info@jamescullimore.dev. We will assess the request and instruct our service providers where appropriate. Transaction and related records may need to be retained to provide paid access, restore purchases, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for records that Apple or RevenueCat must retain.

Your choices and rights

Parents can edit or delete local family information using the app. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights or raise a concern about a child’s information. We may need enough information to identify the relevant purchase or support request without requesting unnecessary information about a child.

Security and changes

Protect your device and parent PIN. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. We will update this page when our processing changes and show the effective date above.
