A little help for your adventure

Game support.

Playing together

Connect all devices to the same trusted Wi-Fi. In version 1.1, one player chooses Multiplayer > Host. The others choose Join, find the matching DGTB game under Nearby games and tap it, then choose Join adventure. Android, iPhone and iPad can play together. If a game is missing, choose Search again or use manual address entry. On iPhone or iPad, allow local-network access in Settings. Guest networks or router isolation may prevent devices from seeing each other. Multiplayer connections are unencrypted and have no internet matchmaking.

Keep the host in the game. Leaving or backgrounding can end the session; return to Multiplayer and join again. All players should update to the same current version.

Languages

Open Settings > Language to choose English, German, Spanish, Italian, French, Brazilian Portuguese or Russian. Automatic follows your device language; unsupported languages use English.

Worlds and controls

Worlds and inventory last for the current session. Leaving ends that adventure. Settings are saved. You can use direction buttons instead of the joystick and enable reduced motion and flashes in the game settings.

Optional tips

All gameplay stays free. Tips are repeatable, one-time consumables, not subscriptions or unlocks. If a payment is awaiting approval, check its status in the store before trying again. Refunds and purchase records are handled by Apple or Google.

Contact us

Email info@jamescullimore.dev with your device model, OS and app version, and what happened. Please include accessibility feedback. Do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary personal information about children.
