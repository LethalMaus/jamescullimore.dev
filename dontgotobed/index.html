This started with the boys’ ideas for a game. The characters, the silliness and the things worth trying grew from making something together.

All characters and gameplay stay free. Optional, repeatable one-time support tips sit behind a grown-ups gate. No subscriptions or gameplay advantages.

A few things for grown-ups.

What kind of game is it?

A building and exploration adventure with cartoon monsters, melee combat and arrows, without blood or gore. Consult the store age rating when the game is listed.

Does it save our world?

Each session is a fresh adventure. Settings are saved; leaving a session ends that world and its progress.

What accessibility options are available?

Direction buttons offer an alternative to dragging the joystick. Reduced motion and flashes are available, and system reduced motion is respected. Full nonvisual gameplay has not been verified.

What information is processed?

No game account or real name is needed. Nearby multiplayer exchanges temporary game state. Optional purchases contact the store and RevenueCat after the grown-ups gate. Read the Android or iOS privacy policy.

How can I get help?

Visit game support or email info@jamescullimore.dev.
