The map gives each route a purpose.

Elchem starts with the map in front of you. One route might pass a blacksmith and a resource stop. Another might lead toward an alchemist, an arena, or a beast trail best saved for later.

🧭

What happens on the map

  • Open the map and check the blacksmiths, arenas and trails nearby.
  • Walk the route to collect resources and reach marked encounters.
  • Use recipe books and materials to craft gear, elixirs and upgrades.
  • Prepare soldiers and beasts before taking on harder fights.

Why the detour matters

Your route changes what you find, what you can craft and which fights feel possible. The fantasy stays tied to the streets and paths you already move through.

Every stop can change the next fight

Choose where to walk, gather what the route offers and use it to prepare your army. Some encounters are worth taking now. Others should wait until your gear, beasts and soldiers are ready.

What waits past the next stop

Elchem is not only about covering distance. The reward is what you uncover on the route and how those finds change what you craft, equip or fight.

Beasts and rival forces

Some marked fights are worth taking immediately. Others are better left until your soldiers, beasts and gear are ready.

Elemental resources

Fire, water, air, earth, thunder and ice shape your soldiers, beasts, weapons and equipment.

Weapons and gear

Better gear changes which routes feel safe, which encounters feel possible and what you prepare for next.

Recipe books

A new recipe can make old materials useful and give your next walk a clear target.

Blacksmiths and crafting stops

Useful places like blacksmiths and crafting stops give the map structure, so different routes can lead to different kinds of progress.

An army built from the route

Small finds add up. A short route today can become the gear that makes tomorrow's fight possible.
